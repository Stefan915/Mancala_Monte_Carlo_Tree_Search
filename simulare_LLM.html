Mancala (Kalah) Rules & MCTS Engine

Goal: Capture more stones in your Store (YOU, right) than the AI collects in its Store (AI, left).

  • Counter-Clockwise Sowing: Pick any non-empty pit on your side (P1–P6). Stones drop one-by-one counter-clockwise through P1→P6→YOU→F6→F1→P1, always skipping the opponent's Store.
  • Extra Turn (+1): If your last stone lands directly in your own Store (YOU), you immediately take another turn.
  • Cross-Board Capture (CAP): If your last stone lands in an empty pit on your side and the opposite AI pit (12 - idx) contains stones, you capture both your landing stone and all opposite stones into your Store.
  • End-of-Game Sweep: When either side's 6 pits become empty, the other player sweeps all remaining stones on their side into their Store.

Minimax-UCB1 MCTS: At AI nodes, UCB1 maximizes AI win rate; at Human reply nodes, UCB1 maximizes Human win rate (1 - aiWinRate). Untried extra-turn moves are expanded first, and tactical rollouts prioritize extra turns and captures with an early cutoff at >24 stones.
